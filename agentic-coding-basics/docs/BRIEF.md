# Brief: CRM API

You have been tasked with building your company a new CRM Solution to replace the old, desktop application. The old solution tracked companies, points of contact within those companies and any communication between your company and its client via either email or phone. This is the scope for your MVP.

The application should be a backend web api in a technology of your choice, featuring authentication/authorization via API Key.

Oh, and you have an hour to get something in front of your manager before he presents it to the CEO, so use agentic tooling.

## Requirements

The MVP should be able to demonstrate the following use cases:

- Create, Update and Archive an organisation
- Create, Update and Archive a point of contact for an organisation
- Manage employees at your current organisation
- Assign employees to specific points of contact at organisations
- Allow an employee to track a communication against an organisation, either at org level or with a specific point of contact
- BONUS: Admin users can manage employees that have access, as well as their level of access (Viewer, Contributor, Manager, Admin)
