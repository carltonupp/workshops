## Wiring up the relationships

Building on the previous step, we now are going to implement entities and crud operations for organisations and points of contact, and hook up.

To summarise the relationship:

An employee will "own" an organisation's account. An organisation may have one or more points of contact, as well as communication preferences such as phone or email. An employee will report to a manager, who in turn will have visibility of all of their subordinate's accounts.

Make sure the database entities are implemented, along with any migrations necessary and CRUD operations.

I wouldn't recommend doing this in a single prompt, but instead approaching incrementally in a single session. Once finished you should only have communications left to implement.
